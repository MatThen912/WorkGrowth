Everyday HR, without the paperwork

Cut down on repetitive admin. Employees submit requests in a few steps, and supervisors can approve them on time, from wherever they are.

  • Attendance

    Employees clock in and out from the web or their phone, so you always know who is at work.

  • Leave

    Staff apply for leave online, and managers approve or decline without forms changing hands.

  • Claims

    Submit expense claims in one place and follow each one from submission to approval.

  • Overtime

    Request and approve overtime with a clear record of the extra hours each person works.

  • Mileage

    Declare work-related travel distance without keeping a separate spreadsheet.

  • Reporting

    Give management a clear view of attendance, requests and day-to-day team activity.

How it works

One simple flow for every request, from the person who submits it to the team that keeps the records.

  1. 1

    Employees submit

    Staff send leave, claims, overtime and mileage requests from the web or their phone.

  2. 2

    Managers review

    Supervisors see what is waiting for them, check the details and approve or decline.

  3. 3

    HR stays informed

    HR sees every record and report in one place, with no paperwork to chase.
